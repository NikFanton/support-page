Pro access and purchases

Open the Pro screen and choose Restore purchases using the Apple account that made the purchase. If Pro still does not appear, contact support with the product name and purchase date. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions. Account deletion does not cancel a subscription.

Pending rewards

If a completed session is waiting to sync, keep the same Time Farm account signed in, reconnect to the internet and use Retry on the focus screen. Pending rewards are saved on that device until synchronization succeeds. Avoid removing the app while a reward is pending.

Account and personal data requests

You can delete your account in the app's account settings. Sign in again first if the app asks for recent authentication, then keep a network connection while deletion completes. If deletion was interrupted, follow the recovery screen.

For access, correction or deletion requests, email guchenko.n@gmail.com from your account address where possible. We may ask for information needed to verify ownership. See the privacy policy for service providers and retention details.

Notifications

Focus reminders are optional. Change them in the app's account settings and manage notification permission in iOS Settings → Notifications → Time Farm.
